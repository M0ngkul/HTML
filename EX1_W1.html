Exploring Khmer Culture

Khmer culture, originating from Cambidia, is rich in history and tradition.

Key Aspects of Khmer culture

  • Traditional Cuisine
  • Festivals and Celebrations
  • Art and Architecture
    1. Angkor Wat - A magnificent temple complexand a symbol of Khmer architectural brilliance.
    2. Khmer New year - A major festival marking the start of the year with traditional dances and ceremonies.
    3. Traditional Dance - Includes classical performances such as the Apsara dance, which is deeply rooted in Khmer tradition.
